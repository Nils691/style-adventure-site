Style Adventure: Merge

Made in Latvia by Nils Klavins

Tap furniture to make clothing, merge matching pieces into better ones, and dress up your character with everything you collect.

Every outfit in the game is a real 3D garment rather than a flat picture, so merging your way up a chain unlocks the next one for your closet. Expand your board, fill daily orders, and enter the fashion contest once a day.
