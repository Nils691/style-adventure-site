The short version: we run no servers and receive nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no in-app purchases, and your game progress never leaves your device. The one exception is advertising: the game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob, and Google's advertising software collects a limited set of data - your IP address, how you interact with the ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device identifiers. Ads are never personalised, for anyone, at any age.

1Who this policy is from

This game is developed and published by Nils Klavins, an individual developer based in Latvia. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to that publisher, and "the game" refers to Style Adventure: Merge.

Questions about this policy can go to info@ragazis.com.

2What data is collected

We collect no personal data ourselves. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, or photos. There is no account to create and no way to log in. We operate no server that receives data from the game, so no information about you or your device reaches us at any point.

Google's advertising software does collect data. The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK so it can offer you optional rewarded video ads (see section 5). Google states that this software automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • IP address - which may be used to estimate the general area a device is in
  • In-app interactions - such as opening the game and interacting with an ad
  • Diagnostic information - about the performance of the app and of the ad software
  • Device and account identifiers

Google uses this for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and states that it is encrypted in transit using TLS. This collection is performed by Google, not by us - we never see any of it, and we receive only anonymous, aggregate earnings totals in our AdMob account (for example, how many ads were watched in a day). Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can read Google's own description of what its ad software collects in its Google Play data disclosure.

Please note when this happens: the ad software starts when the game starts and prepares a video in the background, so that a video is ready the moment you ask for one. This means the data above may be collected even if you never choose to watch an ad during that session.

Beyond this, nothing is collected. The game contains no analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or social-network toolkit, and the Unity engine's own analytics, ads, purchasing, and crash-reporting services are all switched off in the build.

3What the game stores on your device

So your progress is still there next time you play, the game writes one save file into its own private storage area on your device. That file holds only your in-game progress:

  • Your level, experience, and in-game currencies (Coins, Gems, Energy)
  • Which clothing items you own, have discovered, have locked, and are wearing
  • Your merge board layout, daily orders, and contest status
  • Your settings: music, sound, vibration, and language
  • How many rewarded videos you have watched today, so the game can keep to its own daily limits
  • A randomly generated 8-character Player ID

The Player ID is a random string created on your device the first time you play. It is not derived from your device, your hardware, or any account, and it is never transmitted anywhere. It exists so you can quote it to us if you write in for support about your save.

This file lives in the game's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it. You can also erase it at any time from inside the game under Settings → Reset Progress.

4Sharing pictures of your character

The game has a Share button that takes a picture of your dressed-up character and hands it to your device's standard Android share sheet, so you can send it to an app of your choosing.

This only happens when you tap Share yourself. The image is created on your device and passed directly to whichever app you pick - it is never uploaded to us, and we never see it. Once you choose an app to share into, what happens to that image is governed by that app's own privacy policy rather than this one.

5Advertising

The game is free, and rewarded video advertising is how it is paid for. We have deliberately kept advertising to the least intrusive form there is, and we have set it up so that it is as safe as possible for younger players.

What we do:

  • Opt-in rewarded video only. A video plays only after you tap a button that tells you what you will get for watching it - for example, Gems or an energy refill. There are no banners, no pop-up interstitials, no ads when the game opens, and no advertising dressed up to look like part of the game.
  • Ads are never required. Every reward a video gives you can also be earned by playing. The game can be played and finished without ever watching one.
  • No personalised advertising, for anyone. Before the ad software starts, we instruct Google to treat every request from this game as child-directed and to serve only content rated G (suitable for general audiences). This means no interest-based or personalised ads are shown to any player of this game, whatever their age, and ad content is restricted to the most family-appropriate tier Google offers. We chose to apply this to everyone rather than ask players their age, so that nobody has to hand over a birthdate and nobody can end up in the wrong bucket.
  • Daily limits. The game caps how many rewarded videos each part of the game will offer per day, and those counts are stored only on your device.

What we do not do: we do not build a profile of you, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell data, and we receive no personal information about you from Google.

In-app purchases: the game contains none. There is no way to spend real money in it, so no payment or billing information is ever involved.

6Your ad choices and controls

Even though this game requests only non-personalised ads, Android gives you controls over advertising that apply across all of your apps:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On most Android devices this is under Settings → Google → All services → Ads, where you can reset the ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops apps from receiving it.
  • Manage what Google shows you at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Simply do not tap the watch-a-video buttons. No video will ever play unless you ask for one.

7Children's privacy

This game is made for a general audience, and we expect children to play it. We designed it accordingly:

  • The game asks for no personal information at all, from anyone, at any age - no name, no email, no birthdate, no account.
  • Every ad request is flagged to Google as child-directed, with ad content capped at rating G, so no child ever receives a personalised or age-inappropriate ad from this game.
  • There is nothing to buy, so a child cannot spend a parent's money.
  • There is no chat, no messaging, no user accounts, and no way for players to contact each other.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, or if you have any other concern about your child's use of this game, please write to info@ragazis.com and we will act on it promptly.

8Your rights

Privacy laws including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you rights to access, correct, delete, export, and object to the processing of personal data held about you.

From us: we hold nothing about you. We receive no personal data from this game, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export, or delete. The only data the game creates is your local save file, and that is entirely under your control: erase it any time via Settings → Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the game.

From Google: the advertising data described in section 2 is collected and held by Google, which acts as its own data controller for it. To exercise your rights over that data, please use the controls in section 6 and the tools described in the Google Privacy Policy. We are happy to help point you in the right direction - just write to us - but we have no access to that data and cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf.

If you are in the EU and believe your data has been handled improperly, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. In Latvia that is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).

9Retention and security

We retain no data about you, so there is nothing for us to store or secure on your behalf. Your save file stays on your own device for as long as the game is installed, protected by Android's standard app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading it.

Advertising data is transmitted to Google over an encrypted TLS connection, and is retained by Google according to its own retention practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

10Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data with anyone. The only third party that receives any data in connection with this game is Google, in the following ways:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK - serves the rewarded videos and collects the data listed in section 2, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play - distributes the game and collects its own data in connection with that distribution, independently of the game itself.

Both are governed by the Google Privacy Policy rather than this one. The game is built with the Unity engine; because Unity's data-collecting services are disabled in this build, the engine sends neither us nor Unity any data about your play.

This website itself uses no cookies, no analytics, and no external fonts or scripts.
